Who sees your data

The other person in your deal

They see your name as it appears on WhatsApp (or your business name), the deal details, and your trust record on Hoolam. If you get paid, the transfer narration shows the deal code.

People who check you

Anyone can ask our chat about a seller by their number and see that seller’s trust record: the number of deals completed, how many buyers were happy, and how long they’ve been on Hoolam. Sellers can see a short record of a buyer before accepting their deal. We don’t show your phone number, bank details or deal contents to people checking you.

The public, only if you choose

If a seller turns on their public page, anyone with the link can see their business name, city, trust record, and any photo and links they added.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we don’t have to keep it by law (see Delete your data);
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including use based on our legitimate interests;
  • take your data with you in a common format;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by turning off your public page; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) if you think we have handled your data wrongly. We’d like the chance to fix it first, so please tell us too.

To use any of these rights, write to {{EMAIL_LINK}} or message us on {{WA_LINK}}. We will reply within 30 days. To protect you, we confirm requests by messaging the WhatsApp number on your Hoolam account.

How we protect it

  • Everything moves over encrypted connections. Messages and payment updates we receive are checked to be really from WhatsApp and our payment partner.
  • Only Hoolam team members who need it can see personal data. They sign in with a password and a second step on their phone, and each sees only what their role allows.
  • Every action our team takes on a deal or account is recorded with their name, and that record can’t be edited.
  • Money can only move to the seller of a deal or back to its buyer.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Under 18s

Hoolam is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 is using it, we will stop their deals safely and delete what we don’t have to keep.
