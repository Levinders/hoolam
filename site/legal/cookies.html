This website counts visits with Google Analytics, but only if you say yes. No ads, and nothing that follows you around the web. Here is what it keeps on your device, and why.

Analytics cookies, only if you agree

We use Google Analytics to learn how people use hoolam.com and our sellers’ Hoolam pages: how many people visit, which pages they read, which buttons they tap (like Start an order or the live chat), the kind of device, and roughly which city or country they’re in. It helps us make the site clearer.

The first time you visit, a small banner asks. Until you tap Accept, Google Analytics sets no cookies and only gets a simple count without them. If you tap “No, thanks”, that stays so. We don’t use analytics for advertising, we don’t use advertising pixels or social media trackers, and we don’t follow you around the web.

You can delete them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, or change your analytics choice with the button above. The website works the same without them; you would just see the banner again, or start a new chat.

Services the page loads

To count visits, every page loads the Google Analytics tag from Google. Google receives the page address, your device and browser details and your IP address, which Google Analytics uses to estimate your city and doesn’t store. Before you accept, it sets no cookies and gets no identifier for you.

To show our fonts, your browser loads them from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as it does for any website using its fonts. Google doesn’t set cookies for this. Our website and pictures are served by Render, which keeps short server logs (including IP addresses) for security.

When you tap a WhatsApp button, you leave our site for WhatsApp, and WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies there.

The Hoolam team’s console

Our staff console, which only Hoolam team members can sign in to, uses one cookie that is strictly necessary to keep them signed in. It isn’t used on the public website.
