This website doesn’t use cookies, ads or tracking. Here is the little it does keep on your device, and why.

No cookies, no tracking

hoolam.com and our sellers’ Hoolam pages don’t set cookies. We don’t use analytics, advertising pixels, or social media trackers, and we don’t follow you around the web.

What is kept on your device

NameWhat it doesHow long
hoolam-themeRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Stored in your browser (local storage), never sent to us.Until you clear your browser’s data

You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. The website works the same without it.

Services the page loads

To show our fonts, your browser loads them from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as it does for any website using its fonts. Google doesn’t set cookies for this. Our website and pictures are served by Render, which keeps short server logs (including IP addresses) for security.

When you tap a WhatsApp button, you leave our site for WhatsApp, and WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies there.

The Hoolam team’s console

Our staff console, which only Hoolam team members can sign in to, uses one cookie that is strictly necessary to keep them signed in. It isn’t used on the public website.

More about your data

Everything else about how we handle your information is in our Privacy policy.
