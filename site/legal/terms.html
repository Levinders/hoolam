What Hoolam does

Hoolam helps a buyer and a seller who may not know each other complete a sale safely:

  • one side starts an order in our WhatsApp chat and both sides see the same summary of the item, price and fee;
  • the buyer pays Hoolam by bank transfer, and we hold the money;
  • the seller sends the item, and the buyer tells us when it arrives;
  • we pay the seller, or, if something goes wrong, our team looks at it and decides who the money goes to.

We also show a short trust record for each seller and buyer, built from their past orders on Hoolam, and sellers can choose to have a public Hoolam page.

Hoolam is not a marketplace. We don’t list, sell, buy, inspect, store or deliver items, and we don’t set prices. We are not a bank, we don’t take deposits and we don’t give loans.
