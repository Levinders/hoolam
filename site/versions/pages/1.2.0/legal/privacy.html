What we collect when you use Hoolam, why we need it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

In short

  • We collect only what a safe order needs: your WhatsApp number and name, your orders, and the bank account you get paid or refunded into.
  • For bigger orders, and for every seller before their payout, we check your ID once with your BVN or NIN. We store only the result, never your BVN or NIN.
  • If you chat with us on our website, we keep your name, WhatsApp number and the chat (with any files you send) for 12 months after it closes.
  • We never sell your details, and we don’t use them for advertising.
  • Our website counts visits with Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • The other person in an order sees what they need to trust you. Your public page is shown only if you turn it on.
  • We keep order and payment records for 7 years because Nigerian law and our payment partner require it. Everything else you can ask us to delete.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time: {{EMAIL_LINK}}.

Checking your ID (BVN or NIN)

We check your ID once, in the WhatsApp chat, when:

  • you start, accept or pay for a single order above {{MAX_DEAL}} (after the check, a single order can be up to {{MAX_DEAL_VERIFIED}}); or
  • you sell on Hoolam: every seller checks their ID before adding a payout account, or before their next payout if they already have one.

You choose your BVN or your NIN, and give the number and your date of birth. We send them to our identity partner, which checks them against the official records and tells us whether they match, with the name on the ID. We store only the result, never your BVN or NIN: whether it passed, the name on your ID, which one you used, its last 4 digits, the date and the check’s reference. We also keep a one-way code made from the number, which can’t be turned back into it, so we can tell when one person uses two accounts (for example, to stop someone earning as an ambassador from their own orders). The chat message that carried the number is blanked once it’s read.

Once you’ve checked your ID, we pay sellers, and refund buyers, only into a bank account in the same name. Sellers who have checked their ID show “Verified seller” on their trust card and public page, meaning their ID and bank account name were checked by Hoolam. It doesn’t show the name on the ID or any number.

You can try 3 times a day. If it doesn’t work, a rep can help you, and our team may check your ID another way and record that they did.

Ambassadors

If you apply to be an ambassador

We keep what you tell us in your application: your name, WhatsApp number, email (if you give it), state, city and community, who you know, where you will share Hoolam, your social handle (if you give it), roughly how many people you reach, whether you sell online, and why you want to join. Our team uses it to decide on your application.

If you become an ambassador

We also keep your ID check result (as for sellers), your payout account, your term, your link name and, if you add one, your photo, and we record who you brought, what you earned on each order and what we paid you. Your link page shows your first name, the area you chose and your photo to anyone who opens it. Your dashboard on go.hoolam.com uses a sign-in code sent to your WhatsApp and a cookie that keeps you signed in on that device for 30 days; we tell you on WhatsApp when someone signs in on a new device.

If an ambassador brought you

The ambassador who brought you sees your first name (or your business name, if you sell), when you joined, how many orders you have completed and what they earned on each, with the order code. They never see your phone number, address, bank details or what you bought. They don’t learn anything about you if you came to Hoolam another way.

Live chat on our website

You can chat with a Hoolam rep on our website. When you send your first message, we ask for your name and your WhatsApp number. We keep:

  • your name and WhatsApp number, and whether that number already uses Hoolam (so a rep can see your orders and help you faster);
  • your messages, the rep’s replies, and any photos or PDFs either of you sends (up to 10 MB each);
  • the page you started the chat on, when you read the rep’s replies, and your rating if you give one.

We use your number for this chat only: if a rep replies after you’ve left the page, we send you one WhatsApp message with a link back to the chat. We don’t add you to any list. Reps see your first name and number; you see the first name of the rep helping you. The team may add private notes to a chat to help each other, and ratings are used to review how well our reps help.

Your browser keeps a random key so it can reopen your chats (see Cookies). We delete a chat and its files 12 months after it closes.

Who sees your data

The other person in your order

They see your name as it appears on WhatsApp (or your business name), the order details, and your trust record on Hoolam. If you get paid, the transfer narration shows the order code.

The rider or driver delivering your order

When a seller sends your order with a dispatch rider or a waybill driver, we send that rider or driver your first name, your WhatsApp number and the delivery location, so they can reach you and hand the item over. They also see the seller’s name and their own delivery fee. They never see your payment details.

People who check you

Anyone can ask our chat about a seller by their number and see that seller’s trust record: the number of orders completed, how many buyers were happy, and how long they’ve been on Hoolam. Sellers can see a short record of a buyer before accepting their order. We don’t show your phone number, bank details or order contents to people checking you.

The public, only if you choose

If a seller turns on their public page, anyone with the link can see their business name, city, trust record, and any photo and links they added, and, once the page has 50 or more views, how many times it has been viewed.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we don’t have to keep it by law (see Delete your data);
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including use based on our legitimate interests;
  • take your data with you in a common format;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by turning off your public page; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) if you think we have handled your data wrongly. We’d like the chance to fix it first, so please tell us too.

To use any of these rights, write to {{EMAIL_LINK}} or message us on {{WA_LINK}}. We will reply within 30 days. To protect you, we confirm requests by messaging the WhatsApp number on your Hoolam account.

How we protect it

  • Everything moves over encrypted connections. Messages and payment updates we receive are checked to be really from WhatsApp and our payment partner.
  • Only Hoolam team members who need it can see personal data. They sign in with a password and a second step on their phone, and each sees only what their role allows.
  • Every action our team takes on an order or account is recorded with their name, and that record can’t be edited.
  • Money can only move to the seller of an order or back to its buyer.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Under 18s

Hoolam is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 is using it, we will stop their orders safely and delete what we don’t have to keep.
