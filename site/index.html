Quick answers

Still wondering?

Do I need an app?

No. It all happens in WhatsApp.

How much does it cost?

2.5% of the price. Never less than ₦300, never more than ₦5,000. Whoever starts the order pays it.

What if my item never comes?

You get your money back.

What if the buyer says no at the gate?

Tell us in the chat. The money stays frozen while a real person sorts it out with you both. Wrong item? The buyer gets it all back.

How do I pay or get paid?

Buyers pay by bank transfer to a one-time account we send for that order. Sellers are paid straight to their bank account.

Who do I talk to if something goes wrong?

A real person, in the same WhatsApp chat. Tap “Talk to a rep” in the menu.
